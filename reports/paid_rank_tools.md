## 1. SEMrush

### Rank-tracking features

- **Position Tracking** monitors keyword rankings daily across locations, devices, and search engines.
- Tracks ranking changes, **SERP features**, and competitor movement.
- Plans include mobile data; higher tiers add multi-location/device tracking and Share of Voice.
- Rank tracking is bundled with keyword research, competitor analysis, site audit, reporting, backlink analysis, and AI visibility tools.
- The platform also tracks visibility in AI search experiences and custom prompts, but traditional rank tracking remains a core SEO feature.

### Pricing tiers

| Plan | Displayed price | Rank-tracking / project limits |
|---|---:|---|
| **SEO Starter** | $117.33/mo billed annually; $139 monthly | 5 websites; 500 keywords tracked daily |
| **SEO + AI Search Starter** | $165.17/mo billed annually; $199 monthly | 5 websites; 500 keywords tracked daily; 50 prompts tracked daily |
| **SEO + AI Search Pro+** | $248.17/mo billed annually; $299 monthly | 15 websites; 1,500 keywords tracked daily; 100 prompts tracked daily; historical SEO data; multi-location/device tracking |
| **SEO + AI Search Advanced** | $455.67/mo billed annually; $549 monthly | 40 websites; 5,000 keywords tracked daily; 200 prompts tracked daily; API integration; Share of Voice |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom limits, tracking frequency, integrations, governance, and support |

### Business model

- Paid SaaS subscription.
- Monthly and annual billing; the pricing page advertises savings of up to 17% for annual billing.
- Additional users and reporting add-ons are available separately.
- Enterprise is sales-led and contract-based.

### Unique selling points

- Broad all-in-one SEO platform rather than a rank-only product.
- Combines rank tracking with competitor research, backlink data, site audits, content tools, reporting, and AI visibility.
- Strong agency workflow and reporting ecosystem.
- Official feature page describes daily tracking across locations, devices, and search engines, including SERP-feature and competitor movement visibility.

**Official sources:**
<https://www.semrush.com/pricing/>
<https://www.semrush.com/features/>

## 2. Ahrefs

### Rank-tracking features

- **Rank Tracker** is included in the plan feature list.
- Tracks a plan-specific number of keywords and provides **SERP history**.
- Supports competitive analysis and project-level monitoring alongside Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, Site Audit, and Web Analytics.
- The pricing page lists tracked-keyword allowances of 750, 2,000, 5,000, and 10,000+ depending on plan.
- Ahrefs emphasizes its proprietary web index and crawler data as the foundation for SEO and AI marketing workflows.

### Pricing tiers

| Plan | Displayed price | Projects / rank-tracking limits |
|---|---:|---|
| **Lite** | ¥19,900/mo | 5 projects; 750 tracked keywords; 6 months historical data |
| **Standard** | ¥38,400/mo | 20 projects; 2,000 tracked keywords; 2 years historical data |
| **Advanced** | ¥68,900/mo | 50 projects; 5,000 tracked keywords; 5 years historical data |
| **Enterprise** | ¥230,900/mo | 10,000+ tracked keywords; custom scale and controls |

The pricing page states that annual billing can save up to 17%. Currency was displayed as Japanese yen in the retrieved page; actual currency and taxes depend on account location and billing configuration.

### Business model

- Paid SaaS subscription.
- Self-service tiers for Lite, Standard, and Advanced.
- Enterprise is sales-led and tailored.
- Optional add-ons and overage charges are available for some usage categories.

### Unique selling points

- Ahrefs' main differentiation is its large proprietary web index, crawler, backlink database, and long-running historical data.
- Rank tracking is integrated with Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, SERP history, competitive analysis, and content/backlink workflows.
- Particularly strong for teams whose SEO strategy starts with backlink and competitor research rather than rank tracking alone.

**Official sources:**
<https://ahrefs.com/pricing>
<https://ahrefs.com/rank-tracker>

## 3. Moz Pro

### Rank-tracking features

- **Rank Tracker** tracks rankings across multiple search engines and locations.
- Plan limits are expressed as tracked keywords per month: 300, 1,500, and 3,000 on the three public tiers.
- Moz Pro combines rank tracking with keyword research, competitive research, SERP analysis, on-page optimization, link metrics, and site crawl data.
- Newer plans also include AI/GEO visibility dashboards, tracked prompts, AI model tracking, and AI content briefs.

### Pricing tiers

- Monthly or annual billing; annual billing is advertised at approximately 20% savings.

### Unique selling points

- Moz-specific authority and link metrics, including Domain Authority/related link intelligence.
- Combines traditional SEO with AI search/GEO visibility and prompt tracking in the current product.
- Positioning is comparatively approachable for small businesses and marketers who want a centralized SEO workflow.

## 5. Rank Ranger / Similarweb

### Rank-tracking features

The current Rank Ranger domain redirects to a Similarweb-branded SEO tools experience. The current official page describes Rank Tracker capabilities as follows:

- Monitor visibility across traditional SERPs and **AI Overviews**.
- Follow daily ranking trends for important keywords.
- Track daily click trends to measure traffic impact.
- Analyze visibility across SERP features and identify opportunities to rank in features such as AI Overviews.
- Use Similarweb data for keyword research, zero-click analysis, competitor benchmarking, and search-intent validation.

### Pricing tiers

The current official Rank Ranger/Similarweb pricing page does **not** expose public numeric tiers for Rank Ranger. It presents Similarweb Web Intelligence packages and directs business/enterprise buyers to sales for a custom package.

- Public tier prices: **not listed** on the current official page.
- Business/enterprise package: custom, sales-led pricing.
- Free trial or demo options are offered, but package capacity and price are configured with sales.

Older third-party reviews report historical Rank Ranger plans and a base price around $79/mo for 500 keywords, but those figures could not be verified on the current official pricing page and should not be treated as current official pricing.

### Business model

- Current offering is a sales-led subscription/platform package.
- Similarweb's official pricing page states that business and enterprise packages are custom and that paid packages unlock deeper data, timeframes, countries, devices, and workflows.
- APIs, data feeds, MCP, custom dashboards, and managed services are positioned as add-ons or enterprise capabilities.

### Unique selling points

- Similarweb's differentiator is real-user digital intelligence and competitive traffic data rather than rank tracking alone.
- The current page advertises 10,000+ global brands, 100M+ websites, 190 countries, and 6B+ keywords.
- Rank tracking is combined with traffic benchmarking, zero-click metrics, AI Search Intelligence, and competitor analysis.
- Best suited to teams that want rank visibility interpreted alongside market, traffic, and competitor data.

**Official sources:**
<https://www.rankranger.com/>
<https://www.rankranger.com/seo-tools/rank-tracker>
<https://www.similarweb.com/packages/web/>

## 6. AccuRanker

### Rank-tracking features

- Dedicated rank tracker focused on accurate, real-time/daily keyword updates.
- Professional plan includes daily updates, keyword research, search intent, tagging, advanced filtering, Google Search Console and Google Analytics integrations, reporting, and export.
- Expert adds dynamic tagging, Tag Cloud, AI CTR, AI Search Volume, Looker Studio/Google Data Studio integration, and unlimited read API.
- Enterprise adds Google BigQuery, unlimited write API, raw SERP HTML access, enhanced speed, unlimited users and domains, and a dedicated customer success manager.
- The product also offers AccuLLM for AI visibility, MCP access, SERP analysis, forecasting, and API/integration workflows.

### Pricing tiers

| Plan | Price | Keyword allowance |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Professional** | $224/mo | 2,000, 3,000, or 5,000 keywords |
| **Expert** | $764/mo | 10,000, 15,000, 20,000, or 25,000 keywords |
| **Enterprise** | Contact sales | More than 25,000 keywords; custom limits |

Annual payment receives a 10% discount. Plans can be changed, upgraded, downgraded, or cancelled without a fee.

### Business model

- Paid SaaS subscription.
- Self-service Professional and Expert plans.
- Monthly or annual billing; enterprise is sales-led.
- Pricing is primarily keyword-volume based, with capabilities expanding by tier.

### Unique selling points

- Purpose-built rank tracker rather than a general SEO suite.
- Emphasis on fast, accurate, daily/real-time keyword updates.
- Strong tagging, filtering, reporting, API, and white-label/agency-oriented workflows.
- AccuLLM and forecasting extend the product from rank monitoring into AI visibility and business-impact reporting.

**Official sources:**

## Practical Selection Notes

- **Best broad SEO suite:** SEMrush or Ahrefs. SEMrush is stronger as a general marketing/SEO workflow platform; Ahrefs is especially strong for backlink and competitor research.
- **Best specialized rank tracker:** AccuRanker, particularly when daily accuracy, tagging, reporting, and API access matter more than having a full SEO suite.
- **Best budget/high-volume position checks:** Serpstat, especially the Team and Agency plans.
- **Best all-in-one value for agencies/local SEO:** SE Ranking.
- **Best for traffic intelligence plus rank context:** Rank Ranger/Similarweb, but current pricing is custom and must be confirmed with sales.
- **Best approachable mainstream SEO platform:** Moz Pro, especially for small businesses that want rank tracking plus Moz authority/link metrics.

## Research Limitations

- The configured `web_extract` provider was unavailable during this run, so official pages were fetched with `curl` and parsed locally.
- Prices and plan names are volatile; verify the vendor's current checkout page before purchasing.
- Rank Ranger's current official site is redirected/branded through Similarweb, and no current public numeric Rank Ranger pricing tiers were available on the official page.
- Some pages localized prices to Japanese yen; currency conversion, taxes, and regional billing terms are not included.
